Abstract
LEADS is a web-based real estate intelligence and outreach platform built for the Pakistani property
market. The platform operates as an active business tool rather than a passive listing board. Its primary
function is to help real estate professionals and investors identify high-demand property categories
using AI-driven market trend analysis, find matching seller leads through targeted on-demand
scraping of four competing platforms, engage those sellers through structured WhatsApp outreach,
collect property details through category-specific digital forms, analyse the collected data using large
language models to generate valuations and AI-enhanced listing descriptions and manage the
resulting warm leads through a structured campaign and seller dashboard.
Unlike every major Pakistani property platform today, including Zameen.com, OLX Pakistan,
Graana.com, Ilaan.com which function as passive listing boards where sellers post and wait
indefinitely, LEADS actively works the market on behalf of its users. The system scrapes publicly
available listing data from all four platforms continuously, builds a market intelligence layer that
computes listing velocity, average days on market and price movement per category and city and uses
this computed intelligence to surface what is currently trending. Users then trigger on-demand
targeted scrapers with specific filters, review AI-generated seller outreach messages before sending,
collect structured property data through WhatsApp-delivered forms and receive warm lead
notifications when sellers respond. The platform also includes a listing management system where
confirmed properties can be published for buyers to browse and discover. To the best of our
knowledge, LEADS is the first platform in Pakistan to combine real-time market intelligence
computed from multi-platform scraped data, AI-powered seller outreach drafting, structured property
data collection, warm lead detection via WhatsApp webhook and an integrated listing management
system in one connected pipeline. It does not just publish a listing and walk away it actively works
for both the seller and the buyer from the moment a listing goes live.

2. Problem Statement
Real estate agents and sellers in Pakistan currently have no platform that actively helps them to find
serious buyers, they are completely dependent on buyers finding them first. On every major property
platform in Pakistan, including Zameen.com, OLX Pakistan, Graana.com and Ilaan.com, the process
is the same, a seller posts a listing and keep waiting, sometimes for weeks or even months, without
receiving a single genuine inquiry. These platforms do nothing after a listing goes live as there is no
outreach, no follow-up and no system that matches the listing to buyers who are looking for that type
of property. Sellers also have no way of knowing whether their asking price is too high or too low
compared to what similar properties are currently listed for on other platforms. On top of this, there
is no identity verification on most platforms that means anyone can post a list anonymously and fake
listings are very common. OLX Pakistan, for example, publishes listings immediately with no review
at all, making it easy for unverified sellers to mislead buyers. Even the platforms that review listings
before publishing, the quality of descriptions is often poor, with missing details, misleading categories
and prices that do not reflect the actual market trends. Buyers waste a significant amount of time
visiting properties that do not match their need. Sellers with legitimate properties get buried under
low-quality and fake listings, making it much harder for the right buyers to find them. Real estate
agents face the same problems and spend a large part of their working day manually searching for
leads, sending messages one by one and posting listings across multiple platforms with no automation
or intelligence to support them.

3. Problem Solution/Objectives of the Proposed System
LEADS replaces the passive, manual and fragmented process of Pakistani real estate with a
connected, AI-powered and automated pipeline. The system operates across five distinct stages:
market intelligence, targeted scraping, seller outreach and data collection, buyer outreach and warm
lead detection and listing management. Each stage is implemented as one or more dedicated modules
with clearly defined inputs, outputs and technical implementation. In the first stage, LEADS computes
what is trending in the property market by continuously analysing scraped listing data from four
platforms. For each category and city combination, the system tracks listing velocity, average days
on market and price movement. These three signals are combined into a demand score per category
per city and passed to a large language model to generate a human-readable trend recommendation.
This gives users, including those with no prior real estate experience, a data-driven starting point for
their investment decisions. In the second stage, once a user selects a category, city and price range,
LEADS launches a targeted multi-agent scraping run. Rather than one scraper trying to collect
everything, the system launches one Celery worker per platform, each assigned a different proxy IP,
each collecting a fixed maximum of records in parallel. The four workers run simultaneously, their
results are cleaned, deduplicated and merged and the top matching listings are surfaced to the user for
review. In the third stage, the user selects a listing they want to pursue. The system drafts a
personalised WhatsApp outreach message and a terms-and-conditions summary for the user to review
and approve before sending. When the seller responds, the system sends them a category-specific
property data collection form via WhatsApp. The seller fills the form once, providing all required
property details, which the system then passes to a large language model to generate a fair market
valuation, identify the property's hot selling features, flag any issues, confirm the correct category
and produce an AI-enhanced listing description. In the fourth stage, the system uses the confirmed
property details to run a second targeted scraping pass to identify potential buyers: people who are
active on the same platforms in the same category and price range. The system drafts a WhatsApp
campaign for user review and approval, sends it and monitors all replies through a WhatsApp Cloud
API webhook. Any reply, including a thumbs up or a short acknowledgement, is classified as a warm
lead, routed to the seller dashboard and triggers an automated follow-up message draft for user review.
After the follow-up is sent, the system's automated role ends and the agent takes over manually. In
the fifth stage, once a property is confirmed and its AI-enhanced listing is ready, it is published in
LEADS' integrated listing management system where buyers can browse, filter and contact sellers
directly. Any buyer inquiry from this system is also surfaced on the seller dashboard.

6. Scope
LEADS is a web-based real estate platform built for the Pakistani market that covers everything
happening in the pre-transaction phase, from market intelligence to active lead generation and listing
management. The platform serves real estate agents, investors and internal administrators, while
external buyers interact with properties through a centralized listing hub where every user must first
verify their identity via a two-step OTP process. The market intelligence system continuously scrapes
Zameen.com, OLX Pakistan, Graana.com and Ilaan.com using Scrapy, Playwright and parallel proxyassigned Celery workers, cleaning raw records to calculate listing velocity, days on market and price
movements before sending a computed demand score to GPT -4o mini to generate actionable trend
recommendations. When a target property is selected, the platform handles seller outreach via user approved WhatsApp messages, collects structured property details through automated forms and
passes the input to an LLM to generate fair market valuations, highlight hot features, correct
categories and write enhanced listing descriptions. To connect with potential buyers, LEADS
executes a second targeted scraping pass, drafts approved WhatsApp outreach campaigns, monitors
replies through webhooks, automatically classifies positive responses as warm leads and generates
follow-up drafts for agent handover. Once approved, listings publish to LEADS' verified listing
management hub, enabling buyers to filter properties, save favorites, set alerts and contact sellers
directly. LEADS operates strictly as a pre-transaction tool, avoiding payment processing, legal
documentation or financial intermediation and launches exclusively in Pakistan in English.

7. Modules
Module-01 User Authentication

This module controls identity verification and access for all users. It is implemented as a set of Next.js API
routes backed by Prisma ORM on PostgreSQL. OTPs are hashed using bcrypt before storage never stored as
plain text. JWTs are issued in HttpOnly cookies on successful login. Phone OTP is delivered via WhatsApp
Cloud API rather than SMS, as WhatsApp penetration in Pakistan significantly exceeds SMS reliability for
this use case.
FE1: Accept a registration request collecting full name, email address, phone number and role selection.
Validate all fields with Zod before any database operation.
FE2: Generate a 6-digit OTP, hash it with bcrypt, store it with a 10-minute expiry in the emailOtpHash
and emailOtpExpiresAt fields of the User model and deliver it to the user's email via SendGrid.
FE3: Generate a second 6-digit OTP, hash and store it in phoneOtpHash and phoneOtpExpiresAt and
deliver it to the user's phone via WhatsApp Cloud API after email OTP is successfully verified.
FE4:Activate the account only after both OTP hashes are verified within their expiry windows. Phone
OTP is optional at registration but required before the user can initiate any outreach action.
FE5: Enforce a rate-limited OTP resend mechanism using the otpAttempts counter in the User model.
Block resend after the configured maximum attempt count is reached.
FE6: Authenticate returning users via email and password, verify the bcrypt hash, check isEmailVerified
before granting access and issue a signed JWT stored in an HttpOnly cookie.
FE7: Apply role-based access control at the middleware layer, routing ADMIN users to the admin panel,
SELLER users to the seller pipeline and BUYER users to the listing discovery interface.
FE8: Support a secure password reset flow via a time-limited signed reset token delivered by email.
FE9:Verify that the new password does not match the existing bcrypt hash before updating.
FE10: Invalidate the session and clear the JWT cookie on user-initiated logout.

Module-02 Market Intelligence Engine
This module is the analytics brain of LEADS. It runs as a scheduled Celery Beat task that processes
the continuously accumulated MongoDB collection of scraped listing records. For each property
category and city combination, it computes three demand signals and passes the results to a large
language model to generate a human-readable recommendation.
FE1: For each category and city combination present in the MongoDB scraped records, compute listing
velocity: count the number of new records with a scraped_at timestamp in the last 7 days versus the 7
days before that, expressed as a percentage change.
FE2: Compute average days on market as a proxy for sell-through speed: for each category and city,
calculate the mean age in days of all active listings (difference between scraped_at and current date),
where a lower average indicates faster turnover and higher demand.
FE3:Compute price movement: calculate the mean price_numeric of records scraped in the last 14 days
versus the prior 14-day window for each category and city and express the change as a percentage.
FE4: Combine the three signals into a composite demand score using configurable weights stored in the
admin settings table in PostgreSQL. Store the computed demand scores per category per city in a
dedicated analytics collection in MongoDB with a timestamp.
FE5: Pass the top-ranked category and city combinations with their demand scores, velocity figures and
price movement percentages to Google Gemini 2.0 Flash via a structured prompt instructing the model to
generate a concise, plain-language investment recommendation based on this data.
FE6: Store the LLM-generated recommendation text alongside the computed scores in MongoDB.
Surface the recommendation and the underlying score breakdown on the user dashboard, refreshed after
each analytics cycle.
FE7: Run the full analytics and recommendation pipeline on an admin-configurable Celery Beat
schedule, defaulting to once every 24 hours and log job outcomes to the admin audit log.

Module-03 Targeted Scraping Engine:
This module performs on-demand, targeted, multi-agent scraping triggered when a user selects a
property category, city and price range to pursue. Rather than running a single scraper across all pages
of all platforms, the system launches one independent Celery worker per platform, each assigned a
unique IP address from the proxy pool, each configured with a maximum record count. All five
workers run concurrently. This architecture distributes request load across IPs and platforms,
significantly reducing detection risk while completing the full data collection in a fraction of the time
a sequential approach would require.
FE1:Accept a scraping request from the user dashboard containing category, city, minimum price and
maximum price as parameters. Validate these parameters and pass them as arguments to the multi-agent
launcher.
FE2: Launch one Celery task per platform (Zameen.com, OLX Pakistan, Graana.com, Ilaan.com)
simultaneously. Each task is assigned a unique proxy IP from the Redis-backed proxy pool and a
configurable maximum record count, defaulting to 2,500 records per agent.
FE3: Each agent runs a custom Scrapy spider with the category, city and price range passed as search
parameters. Scrapy handles Zameen, OLX .

FE4: Playwright handles Graana and Ilaan. Each spider applies randomized request delays between 3

FE5: Clean raw records through the shared cleaning pipeline: strip HTML artifacts, normalize price to

FE9: Log each agent's outcome (records fetched, records rejected, records deduplicated, errors,

Module-04 Seller Outreach Agent:
This module handles all WhatsApp communication directed at sellers identified through the targeted
scraping engine. Every message that goes out requires explicit user review and approval. No outreach
is sent automatically. The module uses the WhatsApp Cloud API for dispatch and tracks delivery
status per message.
FE1: When the user selects a listing to pursue, retrieve the seller's phone number, name and listing
details from MongoDB. Use these to populate a structured prompt sent to GPT-4o mini, which returns a
personalized outreach message draft and a terms-and-conditions summary including the commission
structure.
FE2: Display the AI-drafted message and T&C summary to the user in a review interface showing the
original seller data alongside the drafted content. The user can edit any part of the draft before approving.
FE3: On user approval, format the phone number to WhatsApp-compatible international format (adding
92 prefix, removing leading zero) and dispatch the message via WhatsApp Cloud API v21+ using the
configured WABA-approved message template.
FE4: Log the dispatch event to PostgreSQL: seller_id, listing_id, message_type, dispatch_timestamp,
delivery_status (SENT, DELIVERED, READ, FAILED). Update the delivery status in real time via
WhatsApp webhook callbacks.
FE5: When the seller replies to the outreach message, detect the reply via the WhatsApp Cloud API
webhook (M-07 handles reply classification for buyer outreach; seller replies are handled here), record
the reply content and trigger M-05 to generate and send the property data collection form.
FE6: If message delivery fails, retry once via WhatsApp after 30 minutes and mark the lead as
UNDELIVERED after the retry fails. Display the undelivered status on the seller dashboard.

Module-06 AI Property Analyzer:
This module processes the structured property data submitted through M-05 using large language
model to generate actionable intelligence. It produces a market valuation, identifies selling points and
issues, verifies the property category and generates an AI-enhanced listing description. All outputs
are shown to the user before any further action is taken.
FE1: Retrieve the submitted form data for a property from PostgreSQL and combine it with the relevant
market intelligence data from MongoDB: comparable listings in the same category, city and size range
with their prices and listing ages.
FE2: Construct a structured prompt containing the full form data and market context and send it to
Google Gemini 2.0 Flash or GPT-4o mini (configurable per admin settings) requesting: (a) fair market
price estimate with minimum, maximum and recommended values, (b) three to five key selling features
of the property, (c) any issues or weaknesses that could affect the sale, (d) the most appropriate property
category based on the submitted details.
FE3: Construct a second prompt using the form data and the LLM's analysis output and send it to the
LLM requesting an AI-enhanced listing description of minimum 150 words, written in professional
English, structured to highlight the property's selling features and location, suitable for direct publication.
FE4: Store all LLM outputs (valuation range, selling features, issues, category recommendation,
enhanced description) in PostgreSQL linked to the seller and listing records.
FE5: Display all LLM outputs to the user in a structured review interface. The user can accept the
outputs, edit any section or request a regeneration before proceeding.
FE6: On user approval, mark the property as analysis-complete in the database and make it available for

FE7: Handle LLM API errors gracefully by logging the error, notifying the user with a retry option and

Module-07
Buyer Outreach and Warm Lead Detection:
This module handles the buyer side of the outreach pipeline. It triggers a second targeted scraping
pass using the confirmed property's details as filters, drafts a WhatsApp campaign for user review,
dispatches it via the WhatsApp Cloud API, monitors all replies through the webhook, classifies each
reply, routes warm and hot leads to the seller dashboard and auto-drafts a follow-up message for user
review. After the follow-up is sent, the system's automated responsibility ends.
FE1: On user initiation of a buyer outreach campaign, call M-03's multi-agent scraper with the
confirmed property's category, city and price range as filters, requesting records that represent active
buyers (people listing properties in the same segment, indicating they are active market participants).
FE2: After the scraping run completes, apply the lead scoring model: score each record on buyer intent
signal strength (NLP analysis of listing text using Hugging Face Transformers), budget alignment (price
of their listing relative to the property's asking price), category compatibility (category match score) and
recency (decay-weighted score based on listing age). Combine scores into a composite using adminconfigurable weights.
FE3: Filter records below the minimum composite score threshold (admin-configurable, default 50/100)
and archive them. Present the passing records to the user ranked by composite score with score
breakdown visible.
FE4: Use the property details and the top-ranked lead records to generate a WhatsApp campaign
message draft via GPT-4o mini. Display the draft to the user for review and editing before any send
action.
FE5: On user approval, dispatch the campaign messages to all approved leads via WhatsApp Cloud API.
Log dispatch status (SENT, DELIVERED, READ, FAILED) per lead in PostgreSQL. Display live
campaign stats on the Campaign Dashboard (M-11).
FE6: Monitor all incoming WhatsApp replies via the WhatsApp Cloud API webhook. For each reply,
identify which campaign message it corresponds to and classify it: HOT (contains 'interested', 'yes', 'tell
me more', 'price?' or similar positive intent keywords), WARM (any other reply including thumbs up,
'ok', 'thanks', short acknowledgement), COLD ('not interested', 'wrong number', 'remove me').
FE7: For HOT and WARM classified replies, create a warm lead record in PostgreSQL linked to the
lead, the campaign and the property. Send a real-time notification to the user dashboard and campaign
dashboard.
FE8: Auto-draft a follow-up WhatsApp message for each warm lead using the property details and the
content of the lead's reply. Display the draft to the user for review and approval. On approval, dispatch
the follow-up via WhatsApp Cloud API. After the follow-up is sent, mark the lead as
FOLLOW_UP_SENT and the system's automated involvement with this lead ends.

Module-08 Listing Management System:
This module is the buyer-facing listing platform integrated within LEADS. When a property has been
confirmed, analysed by AI and approved by the user, it is published here. Buyers browse, filter, save
and contact sellers through this system. It is built on the same Next.js frontend as the rest of LEADS
but serves a separate buyer audience. All buyers must complete OTP verification before accessing

8. System Limitations/Constraints
The following section outlines the boundaries and constraints of LEADS that define what the platform does
not cover in this release.
1. LEADS is a pre-transaction platform only. It does not process property payments, handle rental
agreements, manage legal documents or act in any financial or legal intermediary capacity.
2. The platform is limited to Pakistan in this release. Multi-currency and multi-language support are not
included.
3. The platform is in English only. AI listing enhancement and form processing work correctly for
English-language inputs. Urdu submissions may not be processed accurately.
4. The scraping pipeline only collects data publicly visible without authentication. Private messages,
hidden contact details and data behind login walls on any target platform are never accessed.
Facebook Marketplace is excluded because it requires login to view listings.
5. The multi-agent scraping strategy requires a pool of rotating proxy IPs. Without proxy rotation,
concurrent requests from the same IP will be blocked by target platforms. The quality and
availability of the proxy pool directly affects scraping reliability.
6. WhatsApp Cloud API outreach requires WABA-approved message templates. Template approval
can take 24 to 72 hours. Unapproved templates cannot be sent to users who have not previously
messaged the LEADS WhatsApp number. This limits cold outreach to template messages only.
12

Scope Document for LEADS

7. The warm lead detection webhook relies on the WhatsApp Cloud API receiving and forwarding all
replies. Message delivery and reply detection are subject to WhatsApp's platform reliability and rate
limits.
8. The LLM analysis pipeline depends on Google Gemini API and OpenAI API availability. Service
outages, rate limit enforcement or pricing changes may temporarily interrupt the AI property analysis
and trend recommendation features.
9. The lead scoring model is trained on Pakistani real estate listing data available at development time.
Its accuracy will degrade as market conditions change and will require periodic retraining.
10. The system does not verify that a seller legally owns the property they are selling. Sellers are fully
responsible for the accuracy and legal compliance of the information they submit through the
property form.
11. Deal closure and revenue tracking are recorded manually by the user. LEADS does not verify that a
deal actually closed or that the recorded commission amount is accurate.

9. Data Gathering Approach
Requirements for LEADS were gathered through four approaches. A structured technical analysis of
four existing Pakistani real estate platforms Zameen.com, OLX Pakistan, Graana.com, Ilaan.com and
was conducted to map their publicly accessible data fields, confirm scraping feasibility using Scrapy
and Playwright, verify the absence of public comment sections or buyer behavior data and document
each platform's anti-scraping measures, with findings directly shaping the scraper architecture and
the decision to use on-platform buyer behavior rather than externally scraped intent signals.
Unstructured interviews were conducted with real estate agents and property investors from
Islamabad and Rawalpindi, focusing on how they currently identify investment opportunities, how
long it takes to collect complete property details from sellers, how many messages they send before
receiving a serious response and whether an AI-drafted outreach tool would be adopted in their
workflow, with responses consistently identifying manual data collection and the inability to identify
trending categories as their biggest time costs. A questionnaire was distributed among property buyers
asking how they discover properties, what information is most frequently missing from listings they
encounter and whether a platform with AI-enhanced verified listings would be preferred over existing
options, with the majority citing incomplete listing information and fake listings as their primary
frustrations. A technical feasibility study was conducted for each of the five scraping targets to
confirm that category, price, location and seller contact data is publicly accessible, to identify which
platforms require Playwright versus Scrapy and to validate the multi-agent parallel architecture as
both technically sound and practically achievable within the project timeline..

10. Project Contribution
LEADS introduces several technical and conceptual contributions which do not exist in any current
Pakistani real estate platform:
 Computed Multi-Platform Market Intelligence: LEADS is the first Pakistani platform to
compute real-time market demand scores for property categories and cities by continuously
analysing listing velocity, average days on market and price movement across five competing
platforms simultaneously. No existing platform provides this intelligence to users.


Multi-Agent Parallel Scraping Architecture: Rather than running a single scraper that can
be blocked and takes hours to complete, LEADS launches one independent Celery worker per
platform, each on a separate proxy IP, each with a configurable record limit, all running in

13

Scope Document for LEADS

parallel. This multi-agent architecture collects targeted, filtered data from five platforms in
minutes and is far more resilient to IP blocking than any single-agent approach.


Human-in-the-Loop AI Outreach Drafting: LEADS uses LLM APIs to draft WhatsApp
outreach messages, terms-and-conditions summaries and buyer campaign messages, all
subject to mandatory user review before dispatch. This human-in-the-loop approach to AIassisted outreach is not present in any Pakistani real estate tool.



Category-Specific Structured Property Data Collection: LEADS delivers categoryspecific property data collection forms to sellers via WhatsApp, with different mandatory
fields per property type. This replaces the back-and-forth messaging that currently dominates
an agent's working day with a single structured submission that captures all required data at
once.



LLM-Driven Property Valuation and Description Generation: Submitted form data is
passed to LLM APIs to generate a fair market valuation with minimum, maximum and
recommended prices, identify hot selling features, flag issues, verify property category and
produce an AI-enhanced listing description. This end-to-end property intelligence generation
from structured seller input is novel in the Pakistani real estate context.



Webhook-Based Warm Lead Detection and Classification: LEADS monitors all
WhatsApp replies via the Cloud API webhook, classifies each reply as HOT, WARM or
COLD based on content analysis and routes warm leads to the dashboard with an auto-drafted
follow-up for user review. This automated warm lead detection from WhatsApp campaign
replies is not available in any existing Pakistani real estate tool.



Dual-Channel OTP via WhatsApp: All users must verify both email address and phone
number via OTP before accessing any platform feature. Phone OTP is delivered via WhatsApp
rather than SMS, reflecting the reality that WhatsApp penetration in Pakistan is significantly
higher than SMS reliability.



Role-Based Three-Portal Architecture: LEADS is constructed on the basis of three portals
Seller and Agent Portal, Buyer Portal and Admin Portal. All three portals have completely
different interface, feature set and view of data tailored exactly to the user using that portal.

Contribution Impact
Through integration of AI-driven listing enhancement and enrichment, lead scoring by means of
NLP techniques, automation of multi-channel lead outreach, verification of user identities,
gathering of live intelligence about the market and role-based portal architecture, LEADS changes
the game in Pakistani real estate sector significantly. LEADS will allow sellers to leverage a real
estate platform to their full potential. Rather than relying on traditional platforms where sellers
just list their property and wait to sell it, LEADS will become a complete active seller-centric
platform. With these contributions made, LEADS will be an innovative product indeed.
